# Replit Update Prompt — HonorFirstLife Landing Page Tweaks Based on PPC Team Feedback

We are updating the HonorFirstLife landing page based on PPC team recommendations.

Important correction: the website/brand/domain should be **HonorFirstLife** and **HonorFirstLife.com**. Do not use any incorrect homepage or URL referenced in outside recommendation documents.

The goal is still one primary PPC landing page for now, but it should be optimized more strongly for phone calls, form submissions, trust, mobile conversion, and future expansion into separate product pages.

## Primary Objective

Optimize the landing page to generate qualified leads for Jesse Reiter through:

1. Phone calls
2. Text messages
3. Lead form submissions
4. Callback requests

Phone calls should now be treated as the primary conversion action, with form submissions as a secondary but still important conversion path.

## Overall Landing Page Direction

The page should clearly communicate:

* Jesse helps veterans and families review private life insurance options.
* This is private life insurance guidance, not VA/government coverage.
* The visitor can call, text, or submit a short form.
* The process is simple, respectful, and no-pressure.
* Coverage, pricing, and approval depend on state, age, health, carrier, product, and underwriting.
* No Social Security number, banking information, payment information, VA claim number, Medicare number, or full medical history should be collected on the landing page.

## FAQ Section Update

Add or update FAQs:

### Is HonorFirstLife the VA?

No. HonorFirstLife is a private life insurance resource and is not affiliated with the VA, U.S. government, or any military branch.

### Can I call instead of filling out the form?

Yes. If you prefer to talk, you can call Jesse directly using the phone button on this page.

### What kind of life insurance can Jesse help with?

Jesse can help review private options for final expense, whole life, term life, mortgage protection, Indexed Universal Life, and existing policy reviews.

### Is approval guaranteed?

No. Approval, coverage amount, premium, and product availability depend on age, health, state, carrier, product, and underwriting.

### Do I need to enter my Social Security number on this website?

No. Do not enter Social Security numbers, banking information, payment information, VA claim numbers, Medicare numbers, or full medical history on this website.

### What happens after I submit the form?

Jesse may call or text you based on your selected contact preference to review your goals and discuss what options may be available.

### Can Jesse review a policy I already have?

Yes. Jesse can help review an existing policy and discuss whether it still fits your goals, budget, and family needs.

### Is IUL right for everyone?

No. Indexed Universal Life is not right for everyone. It may offer protection with cash value growth potential, but it also includes policy terms, fees, caps, participation rates, and other limitations that should be explained before deciding.
