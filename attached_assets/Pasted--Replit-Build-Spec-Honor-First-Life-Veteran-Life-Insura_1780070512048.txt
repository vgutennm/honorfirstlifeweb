# Replit Build Spec — Honor First Life Veteran Life Insurance Landing Page

Build a production-ready, mobile-first lead generation website for **Honor First Life** at **honorfirstlife.com**.

This website will be used as the landing page for PPC campaigns managed by an external agency. The first version should focus on **one high-converting veteran life insurance / final expense landing page** designed to generate phone calls, text messages, and form submissions for Jesse Reiter.

The site should be inspired by the simplicity and conversion structure of **vetlifecoverage.com/quote**, but must not copy its branding, copy, images, testimonials, layout exactly, or any non-compliant claims. Use the best design ideas only: simple hero, emotional headline, short quiz-style form, veteran trust positioning, social proof/testimonial placeholders, agent credibility section, clear CTAs, privacy reassurance, and repeated “Get Help / Check Options” calls to action.

## Core Strategy

This should not feel like a generic life insurance website. It should feel like a trustworthy, respectful, veteran-focused private insurance review page.

The target visitor is usually an older veteran, often age 58–78, skeptical of online insurance ads, protective of family, worried about funeral costs, and concerned about being pressured, scammed, or misled.

The site must solve these problems:

1. Make the visitor feel safe.
2. Make it clear Jesse is not the VA or government.
3. Make it easy to call, text, or submit a form.
4. Make the visitor remember why they submitted the form.
5. Set realistic expectations around coverage and approval.
6. Give Jesse better lead quality than generic vendor leads.
7. Capture tracking data for Google Ads, GA4, GTM, and future CRM workflows.

## Important Compliance Positioning

The site must clearly and repeatedly say:

**Honor First Life is a private life insurance resource and is not affiliated with the VA, U.S. government, or any military branch.**

Do not make the site look like a government program.

Do not use official seals, VA-style marks, military branch logos, fake government badges, or anything that implies government affiliation.

Avoid these claims:

* Guaranteed approval
* Lowest rates
* Best rates
* Better than the VA
* Free VA benefit
* Government benefit
* Everyone qualifies
* No medical questions for everyone
* No exam guaranteed
* Veterans get special government rates
* Coverage regardless of health

Use safer language:

* Options may be available.
* Day-one coverage options may be available depending on approval.
* Coverage, pricing, and approval depend on age, health, state, product, carrier, and underwriting.
* A licensed insurance professional can help review private life insurance options.

## Lead Quality Logic

Create hidden internal lead score. Do not show it to the visitor.

Higher priority:

* Licensed state match
* Veteran or spouse/family member of veteran
* Age 50+
* Final expense / family protection goal
* Provides phone
* Chooses ASAP or today
* Gives a clear reason for looking into coverage
* Wants a coverage review
* Requests phone call

Lower priority:

* Outside licensed states
* No phone
* Unclear intent
* Wants only email
* Under 40 and only wants IUL

Do not reject leads automatically based on health, age, income, veteran status, or protected/sensitive factors. Use lead score only for prioritization.

Lead statuses:

* New
* Hot / Call Now
* Contacted
* Appointment Set
* No Answer
* Bad Number
* Not Licensed State
* Not Qualified / Nurture
* Application Started
* Submitted
* Issued
* Cancelled / Chargeback Risk

## FAQ Section

### Is Honor First Life the VA?

No. Honor First Life is a private life insurance resource and is not affiliated with the VA, U.S. government, or any military branch.

### Can veterans qualify for life insurance without a medical exam?

Some private life insurance options may not require a traditional medical exam, but approval, pricing, and coverage depend on age, health, state, product, carrier, and underwriting.

### What if I already have life insurance?

Jesse can help you review what you already have and discuss whether it still fits your goals, budget, and family needs.

Jesse may call or text you based on your preference to review your goals, answer questions, and explain private life insurance options that may be available.

No. Coverage is not guaranteed. Approval, coverage amount, and premium depend on the carrier, state, product, age, health, and underwriting.

### Can Jesse help if I already have VA benefits?

Yes. Jesse can help review private life insurance options outside of VA benefits. Honor First Life is not the VA and does not replace official VA guidance.
